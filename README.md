## Insights
- December's increase in revenue was primarily driven by higher-value coffee equipment, particularly the High-Capacity French Press, Automated Drip Brewer, and Hand Grinder. These products also generated large profit, which shows that the revenue growth was supported by strong sales of higher-priced items rather than volume.
- Profitability is heavily focused in one product category, which contributes 57.3% of total profit. If the performace in this category decreases, the business may have a risk even if this category drives as primary profit.

## Pricing Recommedation
- Discontinue or Increase Prices: Review the following low-margin accessories and merchandise and consider discontinuing them or increasing their prices by 25% or more:
Chemex Filters (100 Pack) — 16.31% margin, the lowest among all products
Minimalist Keychain — 16.35%
Logo Hoodie (Black) — 16.61%
- Required Price Adjustment: Increase the price of the Gooseneck Electric Kettle to bring its Gross Margin Percentage (GMP) back above the 30% threshold.
- Rationale: Rising Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) has significantly reduced profitability, making these products either unprofitable or weak contributors to overall profit.
